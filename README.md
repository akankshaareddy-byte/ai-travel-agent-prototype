# Plan with Mimi — an AI group-trip planner

A clickable prototype for the Razorpay Design NID assignment: an AI-powered way to plan **and book** a multi-day group trip on MakeMyTrip, through conversation instead of search and filters.

**Live prototype:** https://akankshaareddy-byte.github.io/ai-travel-agent-prototype/

> Concept work in MakeMyTrip's visual language. Not affiliated with MakeMyTrip.
> One trip is scripted: Goa, six friends from three cities, about ₹20,000 each.
> Prices, Mimi's replies and the other five friends are simulated. Payment is skipped.

## The journey

1. **Tell me what you want.** Type, talk, paste a link, or tap the Dates / Budget / Place chips. Set any two; Mimi suggests the third.
2. **Three long weekends**, weighed by season, weather and off-season fares. Mimi's pick leads; an over-budget option says by how much.
3. **The group agrees** in a shared chat. Silence isn't a yes.
4. **Lock 1: the flights.** Terms and the full per-head cost appear before anything is booked. Bookings land in the chat as each person books.
5. **The trip tab.** The plan as folders of saved places, one folder per day, with the budget against the target.
6. **Know more.** Open any place and ask Mimi. She has no opinion of her own; she shows who has been — reels, Reddit, YouTube, blogs.
7. **Review and control.** Like, pin, swap (Mimi's pick first) or remove any place, with undo. Move a day and Mimi flags what breaks.
8. **Lock 2: the stay** — and a plan change mid-booking.

## The two edge cases from the brief

- **No options within budget** — set the budget to ₹15K. Mimi shows the gap and three ways to close it.
- **Plans change mid-booking** — book the stay. A friend drops out; Mimi shows the impact before anything is cancelled, and only the five still going re-confirm.
